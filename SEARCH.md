# Search details and coverage

Supplement to the paper *New strongly regular graphs on 1296 vertices from partial difference sets in
F_16 × F_81*. This file records how the search was set up and how far it has got. **The search is still
running.** The numbers below are a snapshot of 12 October 2026 and will be updated.

## Models

G = F_{q_1} × … × F_{q_t} (additive group), v = ∏ q_i. A *model* is a pair (S, σ):

- S is a subgroup of ∏ F_{q_i}^*, acting by coordinatewise multiplication;
- σ is either trivial or one semilinear map (a_i) ↦ (a_i^{p_i^{u_i}}), with (u_i) ≠ 0.

The search looks for (v, k, λ, μ)-PDSs D ⊆ G that are invariant under
K(S, σ) = ⟨S, σ, all multipliers x ↦ tx with gcd(t, v) = 1⟩. Including the multipliers costs nothing, by
Ma's multiplier theorem. It also makes the character sums over K-orbits rational integers, so the character
criterion becomes an exact 0/1 integer program (Section 3 of the paper), which is solved with CP-SAT
(OR-Tools 9.15). Every solution is checked independently with the full identity A² = kI + λA + μ(J − I − A).

## How this extends Kovaľ's cyclotomic search

Kovaľ's search on F_4 × F_81 (preprint, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23210829, §5.4) let S run over the 16 subgroups
of even order of F_4^* × F_81^* ≅ C_240, and the Galois part over the 8 subgroups of
Gal(F_4/F_2) × Gal(F_81/F_3). That gives 128 groups, each solved with one CP-SAT worker for 30 s.

This search extends it in four ways.

1. **Other field products.** It covers every product of two or three fields whose order is the vertex count of
   an open Latin-square-type parameter set (list below). Every open parameter set on v vertices with integral
   eigenvalues and k ≤ v/2 is tested in each model.
2. **All multiplicative subgroups.** S runs over all subgroups generated by at most two elements. For every
   product used here that is all subgroups, because each multiplicative group has rank at most 2 at every
   prime. For F_16 × F_81 the group F_16^* × F_81^* ≅ C_15 × C_80 is not cyclic and has 80 subgroups. S need
   not have even order, because −1 comes from the multipliers.
3. **Independent Galois maps.** σ ranges over the trivial map and all (u_i) ≠ 0. For F_16 × F_81 that is
   1 + 15 choices, so 1280 models.
4. **Staged solving.**
   - Models with at most 120 orbits: 1 worker, 10 s per case.
   - Deep pass, 121–200 orbits (F_16 × F_81 only): 1 worker, 60 s.
   - Rerun of undecided cases: 5 workers, LP linearisation level 2, 120 s.
   - Tier 3: 20 workers, 30 s per case, then a 600 s pass restricted to parameter sets that are still open.

Graphs I.1–I.3 of the paper came from the first pass. Graph II and graphs I.4–I.10 came from the 5-worker
rerun, in models the first pass had left undecided.

"Open sets tested" means open in the copy of Brouwer's table dated 10 October 2026.

**Solutions found.** The only solutions so far are for three parameter sets on 1296 vertices, all in
F_16 × F_81: (1296, 560, 244, 240) and (1296, 592, 268, 272), which were open, and (1296, 595, 274, 272),
which Kovaľ realised independently (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.23296158) with graphs that are not vertex-transitive.
All three are presented in the paper.

Models with more than 120 orbits (more than 200 for F_16 × F_81) were not searched.

## Code and logs

Everything is in `search/`. The scripts need Python 3 with numpy and OR-Tools; run them from inside `search/`.

| file | role |
|---|---|
| `abelian_pds.py` | the 0/1 integer program for K-invariant PDSs in an abelian group (character criterion over orbits) |
| `run_fields.py` | first pass over all models (S, σ) of a product of fields, e.g. `python run_fields.py "2^4,3^4" LOG 4 10 120` |
| `rerun_fields.py` | 5-worker rerun of the UNKNOWN cases |
| `rerun_tier3.py` | tier 3: 20 workers, 30 s (pass A) then 600 s (pass B) |
| `glclasses.py`, `fusion.py` | helpers: field arithmetic, exact SRG check and graph6 output |
| `open_sets.tsv` | the open parameter sets of Brouwer's table as of 10 October 2026 (the targets) |
| `logs_snapshot_2026-10-12.tar.xz` | all search logs as of 12 October 2026 (one line per model and parameter set: model, parameters, status, number of orbits, time) |

Every reported solution was rebuilt and checked independently; see `../README.md`, section Verification.
