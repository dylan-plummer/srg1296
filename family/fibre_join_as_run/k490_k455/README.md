## What transfers

All three graphs use G = F_2^4 x F_3^4, the same simultaneous order-five action

\[
B(b)=(b_1+b_2+b_3+b_4,b_1,b_2,b_3),\qquad
F(v)=(-v_1-v_2-v_3-v_4,v_1,v_2,v_3),
\]

and the same exact Walsh-character compatibility test. Three seed cross-sets
develop into the fifteen nonzero binary fibres. Only the within-fibre set
and the three seeds change.

| Global degree | Within-fibre SRG | Three seed sizes | Degree calculation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 455 | (81,50,31,30) | 27,27,27 | 50 + 5(27+27+27) |
| 490 | (81,40,19,20) | 36,27,27 | 40 + 5(36+27+27) |
| 525 | (81,30,9,12) | 36,36,27 | 30 + 5(36+36+27) |

For degree 455, the within-fibre set is exactly the complement of the
degree-525 within-fibre graph, with the zero vector excluded. Every cross-set
consists of zero and thirteen projective points together with their negatives.
The degree-490 size-36 seed consists of eighteen projective points and their
negatives, excluding zero. All points are explicit in the certificates.

The common global parameters are Latin-square type

\[
(1296,35m,36+m^2-3m,m^2-m),\qquad m=13,14,15.
\]

This is a verified set of three constructions. No theorem extending it to
every m or to other vertex counts is asserted.

## Exact reductions before a target join

`audit.py` tests all 256 possible inverse-closed C5-invariant within-fibre
sets, and the exact quotient and character-rank conditions, for every open
1296-vertex row in the saved live table. Only degrees 455, 490 and 525 survive
in this particular Cayley ansatz. The `fibre_spectrum` counts in `audit.json`
use forty projective characters, so each corresponds to two nonzero ternary
characters.

For a target with restricted eigenvalues r,s, the quotient trace must satisfy

\[
16d=k+15s+(r-s)j,
\]

where d is the within-fibre degree. Here d is a multiple of ten and the
positive quotient multiplicity j is one of 0,5,10,15. For the nonzero ternary
characters, the rank 16(e-s)/(r-s) must be an integer between 0 and 16.
The surviving fibres have just two ranks: four and eight.

`reductions.py` computes the centralizer actions explicitly. They reduce the
eight degree-455 fibres to one representative, and the six degree-490 fibres
to two representatives. The quotient choices form a single class for each
target. Thus there is one essential fibre/quotient case for 455 and two for
490 before choosing local cross-sets. These are reductions of the stated
symmetry family, not a classification of all SRGs.

The complete local moment-code catalogue was generated by a 20+20
meet-in-the-middle enumeration, without the old five matching restrictions.
It took 3.85236 seconds. An independent dynamic programme confirms the raw
counts: 365080 thirteen-point sets and 2677740 eighteen-point sets. The three
local bounds leave nonempty domains in all three cases.

We then imposed five positive-rank matching channels, as in the successful
degree-525 construction. This is an optional, productive restriction; it is
not a necessary property of every graph in these parameter rows. The signed
Walsh tables contain 140 rank-four and 870 rank-eight patterns. There is no
separate unsigned-to-signed uniqueness assumption in these new joins.

## Successful joins and independent verification

| Target degree | Three domain sizes after filtering | Tested pairs before success | Join time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 455 | 48870,48870,241 | 58 | 0.7565 s, including domain projection and indexing |

The first degree-455 matching choice and the first tested degree-490 fibre
and matching choice succeeded. The remaining cases were not exhausted.
`complete_join: false` records stopping on success.

Each candidate first passed a direct exact difference-count check on its
connection set. The standalone builder then wrote the adjacency matrix and
both graph6 files. A separate standard-library verifier checked every degree,
every one of the 839160 unordered vertex pairs, the complete PDS identity,
every matrix row against the CRT connection set, and the complement. NetworkX
independently decoded each graph6 and matched all adjacencies. Isolated
standalone rebuilds reproduced all data-file hashes and passed verification.

The three primary seed masks for 455 are 0x415000b873, 0xc8308c40d4,
0x18a22ee4. For 490 they are 0x1f4409f9c2, 0x60bd20306, 0xc90d90492.
Mask indices refer to the saved forty-point order; the certificates also
give explicit points so reproduction does not depend on that order.

## What does not transfer directly

All 4550 order-36 subgroups of G were checked. None has its nonzero elements
contained in the degree-525 connection set, and none is disjoint from it.
Thus the usual subgroup-coset clique/coclique spread modification is not
available directly from that graph. This does not exclude non-subgroup
spreads. All 4096 unions of twelve natural pieces of the degree-525 set and
its complement were also checked; they produced no further open parameter
row beyond the graph and its complement.

The degree-420 and degree-385 rows fail the trace condition for the current
order-five action, even before a local search. Reaching those rows requires
changing the symmetry. No parameter nonexistence conclusion follows.

The recommended follow-up is now structural: explain the compatible seed
sets, seek a uniform description of the three rows, and then derive a
different symmetry for the remaining targets. Increasing solve budgets in
the current C5 model cannot reach the rows its trace condition excludes.
