Strongly regular Cayley graphs on F16 × F81

srg(1296, 560, 244, 240)

Three parameter sets on 1296 = 36² vertices, eighteen pairwise non-isomorphic graphs: (1296, 560, 244, 240) and (1296, 592, 268, 272) had no known construction, and (1296, 595, 274, 272) was realised independently by Kovaľ with graphs that are not vertex-transitive. Each is a Cayley graph: a vertex is a pair (a, b) with a ∈ F16, b ∈ F81, and two vertices are joined when their difference lies in a connection set D. Pick a graph below; every panel redraws for it.

Parameters
1 · adjacency matrix

Order changes everything

The same 1296 × 1296 matrix in three vertex orders. In field coordinates it looks like woven cloth. Ordering by discrete logarithms (a = αi, b = βj) exposes the cyclotomic stripes. Shuffled, it is indistinguishable from noise, even though every pair still obeys λ and μ.

Move over the matrix to magnify a 54 × 54 window.

Hover the matrix to inspect a cell.
2 · the recipe

The connection set D

All of F16 × F81 as 16 rows (0, α0, …, α14) by 81 columns (0, β0, …, β79). Filled cells are the elements of D. The repeating pattern along each row is the multiplicative symmetry the graph was built from.

Switch to the character view: every non-trivial character sums to one of exactly two values on D, the two non-trivial eigenvalues. That two-valuedness is what makes the graph strongly regular.

3 · network

Count the common neighbours

Each ring is a coset {a} × F81: βj sits at angle 2πj/80, with 0 in the middle. The centre ring is a = 0 and αi sits at angle 2πi/15.

Click a vertex to choose the blue point, click again to choose the orange one. The highlighted dots are their common neighbours; the count always equals λ for adjacent pairs and μ for non-adjacent ones.

4 · inside a ring

One ring of 81 vertices

Between rings the counts are rigid for every graph here: the 16 rings form an equitable partition, so each vertex of ring a has the same number of neighbours in ring a′.

Neighbours a vertex in ring a (row) has in ring a′ (column); rows in the order 0, α0, …, α14.

Found by exact CP-SAT searches for partial difference sets in F16 × F81 invariant under a subgroup of field multiplications plus a Galois map, generalising Kovaľ's construction of srg(324, 136, 58, 56) on F4 × F81. Fields: F16 = F2[x]/(x4+x3+1), F81 = F3[y]/(y4+y3+2). Every graph was rebuilt by a standalone script that checks all 839 160 vertex pairs, and checked again by an independent verifier. Isomorphism classes and automorphism groups by nauty. None of the Latin-square-type graphs is the graph of an orthogonal array.
